Privacy policy

jeremy, a private personal assistant. Last updated 30 September 2026.

Who operates it

jeremy is operated by Petko Pavlovski for his own Google accounts. It has no other users and does not accept any.

What it reads

  • Gmail messages and settings, through the read-only Gmail scope.
  • Google Calendar calendars and events, through the read-only Calendar scope.

It requests no other Google data and has no permission to send, modify or delete anything.

What it does with it

It reads mail and calendar entries in order to summarise them for the account owner and answer his questions about them. That is the only use.

Where the data goes

  • Access tokens and working state are kept on a single privately owned computer under the operator's control.
  • To produce a summary, the relevant mail and calendar content is sent to Anthropic's API, where a language model processes it.
  • The resulting summaries are delivered to the operator through Telegram.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Retention

jeremy keeps no separate archive of mail or calendar data. Its conversation history, which can include the content it read and the summaries it wrote, stays on the operator's computer until he deletes it.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page, myaccount.google.com/permissions, by removing jeremy.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/cargopete/jeremy.
