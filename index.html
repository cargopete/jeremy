jeremy

A private personal assistant, run by one person for his own accounts.

What it is

jeremy is an assistant operated by Petko Pavlovski. It reads his own Gmail and Google Calendar so that it can tell him what needs attention: unread mail worth answering, what is on the calendar, and what clashes.

It is not a product or a public service. There is no sign-up, and nobody other than its operator is able to connect a Google account to it.

What it can access

  • Gmail, read-only: it can read messages and settings. It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: it can read calendars and events. It cannot create or change them.
